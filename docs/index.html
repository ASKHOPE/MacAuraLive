Engineered for Performance & Aesthetics

Every feature in MacAuraLive is purpose-built for low power draw and seamless macOS integration.

🎬

Hardware 4K/8K Video Engine

Native AVFoundation video loops supporting H.264, HEVC/H.265, and ProRes. Includes real-time timeline seeking slider with MM:SS precision and 0.25x to 3.0x dynamic playback speed controls.

🤖

AI Wallpaper Workshop

Compose custom live wallpapers with natural language prompts. Connect Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude, OpenAI, OpenRouter, or local offline LLMs (Ollama) with hardware Keychain encryption.

📐

Pixel-Perfect 1:1 Sizing

Original Resolution default mode preserves exact 1:1 pixel dimensions and native aspect ratios without distortion or forced stretching. One-click toggles for Fit, Fill, Stretch, and Zoom.

🖥️

Multi-Monitor & Spanning

Assign independent wallpapers per display or span panoramic wallpapers across multiple monitors. Includes dynamic screen hot-plug detection and individual monitor scaling.

🔒

Independent Lock Screen

Set a custom static wallpaper image for your macOS lock screen while keeping high-performance animated live wallpapers active on your desktop.

⚡

Smart Power Management

Automatically pauses wallpaper rendering when full-screen games or applications are active, or when MacBook runs on battery power, preserving 100% GPU resources.
